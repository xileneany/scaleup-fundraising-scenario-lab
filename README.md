# Scale-Up & Fundraising Scenario Lab

An interactive decision-support prototype exploring how operational growth decisions can affect supplier capacity, cash requirements, and fundraising needs in a scaling startup.

## Business Question

A fast-growing production startup expects demand to increase significantly over the next 24 months.

Management needs to understand:

- Can the current supplier network support projected growth?
- When could capacity constraints appear?
- Which supplier expansion strategy offers the best balance between cost, capacity, reliability, and risk?
- How would expansion affect operating costs and cash requirements?
- How much additional funding could be required to support the selected growth scenario?
- What runway would different fundraising scenarios provide?

The goal of this project is to connect operational planning with financial decision-making in one interactive management tool.

## Decision Flow

Growth assumptions  
→ Capacity requirements  
→ Supplier expansion alternatives  
→ Financial impact  
→ Funding requirement  
→ Runway scenarios  
→ Management recommendation

## Planned Application

The Streamlit application will include:

1. Executive Overview
2. Growth & Capacity Planning
3. Supplier Network Simulator
4. Financial Impact
5. Fundraising & Runway Scenarios
6. Founder Brief

## Data Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio project built with synthetic data and illustrative assumptions.

It does not use confidential, internal, or non-public company information. The financial, operational, supplier, and fundraising figures used in the model are fictional and are designed solely to demonstrate analytical and decision-support methods.
